Stage-II Main Examination

HP Process Server Written Examination (40 Marks)

Under Rule 5 of the HP District Judiciary Rules 2022, the Written Examination carries 40 marks out of the 50-mark final merit (the remaining 10 marks are for evaluation/interview). This stage determines your final appointment!

25 Marks • ~250 Words

Hindi Essay Writing (निबंध लेखन)

Candidates are required to write an essay of about 250 words on one out of three given topics. Topics typically cover regional Himachali issues, environmental conservation, digital governance, or social awareness.

Key Evaluation Criteria: Thought coherence, relevant facts, correct Hindi grammar, paragraph structure, and neat, legible handwriting.

15 Marks • ~100 Words

English Application & Letter Writing

Candidates must draft a formal official letter or application (approximately 100 words). The layout must adhere strictly to formal administrative letter conventions.

Key Evaluation Criteria: Format layout (Sender, Date, Receiver, Subject, Salutation, Body, Sign-off), concise formal expression, and spelling accuracy.

Official Handwriting & Presentation Rule

The statutory rules explicitly specify that special consideration and weightage are given to candidates with clean, neat, and legible handwriting. In judicial court duties, process servers are required to write notices, deliver summons, and record receipt acknowledgments; therefore, clarity of penmanship is an essential evaluated skill.
