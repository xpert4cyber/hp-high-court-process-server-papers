Essential Formal Letter Format

  1. Receiver's Designation & Office: E.g., To, The Learned District & Sessions Judge, District Courts, Shimla, H.P.
  2. Date: Clear formal date line.
  3. Subject Line: Concise, uppercase or capitalized subject (e.g., Sub: Application for three days casual leave - Reg.)
  4. Salutation: Respected Sir / Madam,
  5. Body (~60-70 words): Respectful statement of purpose and dates.
  6. Subscription: Yours faithfully, [Name / Roll No.]
Model Formal Answer

Model Application (Casual Leave)

To,
The Learned District & Sessions Judge,
District Court Complex, Shimla (H.P.)

Date: 15th December 2024

Subject: Application for two days casual leave.

Respected Sir,

Most respectfully, I beg to submit that due to an unavoidable domestic emergency at my native village, I shall be unable to attend to my official duties on the 16th and 17th of December 2024.

All pending summon service acknowledgments assigned to me have been duly deposited in the Nazarat section.

Kindly grant me casual leave for the aforesaid two days.

Thanking you.

Yours faithfully,
Ramesh Kumar
