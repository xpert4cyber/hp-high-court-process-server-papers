Data Transparency

Privacy Policy & Data Practices

HPProcessServerPapers is designed with a strict privacy-first architecture. We do not operate backend servers, require logins, or sell user information.

1. No Server-Side Personal Data Collection

This website is hosted statically on GitHub Pages. We do not maintain any application server, user database, or authentication mechanism. You can use all mock tests, question banks, writing modules, and paper archives without providing your name, phone number, email address, or payment details.

2. Use of Browser LocalStorage

To provide student dashboard capabilities, test history tracking, question bookmarking, and essay autosaving without a backend, the website utilizes your browser's native LocalStorage:

  • hppsp_theme: Saves your preferred visual theme (light or dark mode).
  • hppsp_test_history: Records your screening test scores, marks, and date of attempt.
  • hppsp_bookmarks: Saves questions you marked with the bookmark star icon.
  • hppsp_incorrect_answers: Collects questions you missed in tests for revision drills.
  • hppsp_written_drafts: Saves your in-progress Hindi essay and English letter drafts.

All LocalStorage data resides exclusively on your local device. It is never transmitted across the network to us or any third party. Clearing your browser cache or site data will remove this stored information.

3. Hosting Infrastructure

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you access the website, standard web server logs (including your IP address, browser user-agent, and requested file path) may be processed by GitHub in accordance with their GitHub Global Privacy Statement for security, DDoS defense, and infrastructure integrity.
