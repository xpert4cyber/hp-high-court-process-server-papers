Official Objection Submission Protocol

When provisional answer keys are released by the High Court of Himachal Pradesh, candidates are typically granted 3 to 7 working days to submit representation along with documentary proof from standard textbooks (such as NCERT, standard gazetteers, or statutory rules). Objections without documentary proof are summarily rejected.
