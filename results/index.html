Results, Cutoff Trends & Shortlisting Ratio

Understand how screening test cutoffs and the 1:5 ratio determine entry to the 40-mark Written Descriptive Examination.

The 1:5 Shortlisting Ratio Explained

Under Rule 5(1) of the District Judiciary Staff Rules 2022, candidates who appear in the 50-mark objective Screening Test are ranked in order of marks. For each advertised vacancy in a judicial district, exactly 5 candidates (ratio 1:5) are shortlisted for the Stage-II Written Descriptive Test.

Important: Screening test marks are purely qualifying in nature and are NOT added to the final selection merit!
