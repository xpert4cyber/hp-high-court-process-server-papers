Himachal Pradesh GK Learning Centre

In the 50-mark screening examination, Himachal Pradesh General Knowledge comprises 15 to 20 questions. Review high-frequency facts below before drilling into the practice questions.

Key Rivers & Vedic Names

  • Sutlej: Shatadru / Shutudri (Enters at Shipki La)
  • Beas: Vipasa / Arjikiya (Rises at Rohtang Pass)
  • Ravi: Purushni / Iravati (Rises at Bara Bhangal)
  • Chenab: Asikni / Chandrabhaga (Confluence at Tandi)
  • Yamuna: Kalindi (Enters at Khodar Majri)

Crucial Mountain Passes

  • Chobia Pass: Chamba and Lahaul (4,934 m)
  • Rohtang Pass: Kullu and Lahaul (3,978 m)
  • Baralacha La: Lahaul and Ladakh (4,890 m)
  • Shipki La: Kinnaur and Tibet
  • Kunzum Pass: Lahaul and Spiti (4,551 m)

Historic Fairs & Architecture

  • Minjar Fair: Chamba Chowgan (Maize blossom)
  • Kullu Dussehra: Dhalpur Maidan (Lord Raghunath)
  • Mandi Shivratri: Paddal Maidan (Madho Rai)
  • Masrur Temples: Rock-cut Nagara style in Kangra
  • Hidimba Temple: Wooden Pagoda style in Manali
