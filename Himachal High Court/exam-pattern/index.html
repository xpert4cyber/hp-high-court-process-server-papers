Selection Architecture

HP Process Server Examination Pattern & Scheme

Comprehensive breakdown of the three-stage selection architecture: Screening Test, Written Examination, and Evaluation/Interview.

Selection Stages Overview

Stage Test Type Max Marks Duration Merit Role
Stage-I Screening Test (Objective MCQs) 50 Marks 60 Minutes Qualifying (1:5 shortlisting ratio)
Stage-II Written Test (Descriptive: Essay & Letter) 40 Marks 60 Minutes Forms 80% of final merit list
Stage-III Evaluation & Personality Test 10 Marks — Forms 20% of final merit list
Final Selection Merit Total 50 Marks (40 Written Test + 10 Evaluation/Interview)

Stage-III: 10 Marks Evaluation Breakdown

Under the District Judiciary Staff Rules 2022, the 10 marks for evaluation and interview are strictly awarded as follows:

1. Educational Qualification (Max 3 Marks)

  • 10+2 or equivalent pass: 1 Mark
  • 10+2 with First Division: 2 Marks
  • Any Bachelor's Degree or above: 3 Marks

2. Experience (Max 3 Marks)

Service rendered as a Class-IV employee or in clerical/administrative positions in Government, semi-government departments, or subordinate courts.

3. Personality Test / Viva-Voce (4 Marks)

Assessment of candidate's alertness, general awareness, communication in Hindi/English, and personal suitability for court summon service duties.
