Statutory Framework

HP District Judiciary Selection Process (Rules 2022)

Recruitment to the post of Process Server in the subordinate courts of Himachal Pradesh is conducted strictly in accordance with the 2022 District Judiciary Staff Rules.

Stage 1: Shortlisting

Screening Test (Objective)

50 Marks • 60 Minutes • Bilingual (English & Hindi)
Multiple-choice questions covering HP GK, General Knowledge, 10+2 Mathematics, Science, English, Hindi, and Mental Ability. Used to shortlist candidates in a 1:5 ratio.

Qualifying Nature Take Test →
Stage 2: Core Merit

Written Test (Descriptive)

40 Marks • 60 Minutes
Consists of a Hindi Essay (25 marks, ~250 words) and an English Application / Letter (15 marks, ~100 words). High weightage is awarded to clean, legible handwriting.

Forms 80% Final Merit Practice Writing →
Stage 3: Assessment

Evaluation & Interview

10 Marks Total
Educational qualification (max 3 marks: Graduation gets 3, 10+2 1st div gets 2), Experience in Govt/Courts (max 3 marks), and Personal Interview/Viva-Voce (4 marks).

Candidate Assistance

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential facts regarding eligibility, age criteria, marking rules, and the selection process.

What is the minimum educational qualification for HP Process Server? +

As per the Himachal Pradesh District Judiciary Staff Rules 2022, candidates must possess a 10+2 (Senior Secondary) examination certificate or its equivalent from a recognized Board of School Education or University.

Is there any negative marking in the screening examination? +

No negative marking was prescribed under the 2022 statutory rules or the 18 December 2022 screening examination. Each question carries 1 mark. Candidates are encouraged to attempt all 50 questions.

How is the final merit list calculated? +

Final selection merit is calculated out of 50 marks: combining the Written Test (40 marks: 25 Hindi Essay + 15 English Letter) and Evaluation/Interview (10 marks). The Stage-1 screening test is purely qualifying for shortlisting in a 1:5 ratio.
