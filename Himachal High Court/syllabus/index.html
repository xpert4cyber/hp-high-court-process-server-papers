Official Syllabus: HP High Court Process Server

Governed by "The Himachal Pradesh District Judiciary Staff (Recruitment, Promotion, Control, Conduct, Discipline and Other Conditions of Service) Rules, 2022".

Baseline Statutory Rules Summary

The recruitment for the post of Process Server (Class-IV cadre in District Judiciary) requires candidates to possess a minimum educational qualification of 10+2 from a recognized Board/University. The syllabus consists of two formal testing stages: a 50-mark objective screening test followed by a 40-mark descriptive written examination.

Part A: Screening Test (50 Marks • 60 Minutes)

Shortlisting Ratio: 1:5

Objective Multiple-Choice Questions (MCQs) of 10+2 standard, bilingual in English and Hindi.

1. Himachal Pradesh GK

  • HP Geography: Rivers, Lakes, Mountain Passes, Glaciers
  • HP History: Ancient princely states, Kangra kingdom, Praja Mandal movement, Statehood
  • Districts, demographic Census 2011, literacy rates
  • Culture: Fairs, festivals, folk dances, traditional temples
  • Hydroelectric projects and state administration

2. General Knowledge & Science

  • Indian Constitution & Fundamental Rights
  • Structure of Indian Judiciary (Supreme Court, High Courts, Subordinate Courts)
  • Indian History & National Freedom Movement
  • Everyday Physics, Chemistry, Human Biology, and Vitamins

3. Mathematics (10+2 Standard)

  • Percentages, Profit and Loss, Discounts
  • Ratio and Proportion, Averages
  • Simple Interest and Compound Interest
  • Time, Work, Speed, Distance, and Time
  • Basic Mensuration (Perimeter & Area)

4. General English

  • Parts of speech & correct usage
  • Articles and Prepositions
  • Synonyms, Antonyms, and Vocabulary
  • Idioms, Phrases, and Spelling correction

5. General Hindi (सामान्य हिंदी)

  • वर्ण विचार एवं वर्तनी शुद्धि
  • स्वर एवं व्यंजन सन्धि
  • समास भेद एवं विग्रह
  • मुहावरे, लोकोक्तियाँ, विलोम एवं पर्यायवाची

6. Reasoning & Mental Ability

  • Number Series and Alphabet Series
  • Coding and Decoding
  • Direction Sense and Distance
  • Analogies and Blood Relations

Part B: Written Test (40 Marks • 60 Minutes)

80% of Final Selection Merit

Descriptive examination consisting of Hindi Essay and English Application Writing.

1. Hindi Essay Writing (निबंध लेखन) - 25 Marks

Candidates are required to write an essay of approximately 250 words on one out of three given contemporary, environmental, regional, or social topics. Evaluated for expression, vocabulary, flow, and handwriting.

2. English Application / Letter - 15 Marks

Candidates are required to write a formal application or letter of approximately 100 words. Evaluated for layout format, formal salutation, clarity, and grammatical precision.
