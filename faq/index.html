Candidate Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Authoritative answers grounded in the Himachal Pradesh District Judiciary Staff Rules 2022.

1. What is the prescribed educational qualification for Process Server? ▾

Under Rule 5 and Schedule-II of the HP District Judiciary Rules 2022, a candidate must have passed the 10+2 (Senior Secondary) examination from a recognized Board of School Education or University.

2. What is the age limit for HP Process Server recruitment? ▾

The general age bracket is 18 to 45 years as on the first day of January of the recruitment year. Relaxations in upper age limits are admissible to SC, ST, OBC, PwD, and Ex-Servicemen categories per HP Government orders.

3. Does the Screening Examination have negative marking? ▾

No negative marking was prescribed under the 2022 rules or applied during the 18 December 2022 screening exam. Candidates receive 1 mark for each correct answer and 0 for incorrect or blank answers.

4. Are screening test marks added to the final merit list? ▾

No. The screening test is purely a shortlisting mechanism used to qualify candidates in a 1:5 ratio for the Written Test. The final appointment merit is calculated out of 50 marks combining the Written Test (40 marks) and Evaluation/Interview (10 marks).

5. What are the duties of a Process Server in District Courts? ▾

A Process Server is a judicial subordinate officer responsible for serving legal summons, notices, warrants, and orders issued by civil and criminal courts to litigants, witnesses, and respondents, and submitting verified return service reports to the Nazarat branch.
