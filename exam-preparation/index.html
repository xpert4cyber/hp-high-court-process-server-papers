Strategic Roadmap

60-Day Study Plan for HP Process Server Exam

A practical, phase-wise preparation plan designed to balance screening test MCQs with descriptive written essay and application practice.

Days 1 to 20

Phase 1: Foundation & HP GK Focus

Master the physical geography of Himachal Pradesh (rivers, passes, glaciers), basic Indian Constitution, and elementary arithmetic. Review the authentic 2022 question paper to understand question patterns.

Days 21 to 40

Phase 2: Language & Written Practice

Drill Hindi grammar (sandhi, samas, muhavare) and English grammar. Begin writing 2 Hindi essays and 2 English applications per week using our live word-counter editor. Focus on clear, neat handwriting.

Days 41 to 60

Phase 3: Timed Full-Length Mocks

Take full 50-mark timed mock tests under strict 60-minute constraints. Review incorrect answers on your dashboard and revise high-frequency HP GK facts.

Standard Recommended Reference Books

Himachal Pradesh GK:
Himachal Darpan (Mian Goverdhan Singh) or The Wonderland Himachal Pradesh (Jag Mohan Balokhra).
General Hindi:
Samanya Hindi by Dr. Hardev Bahri or Lucent Samanya Hindi.
General English:
High School English Grammar & Composition (Wren & Martin).
Elementary Mathematics:
Quantitative Aptitude for Competitive Examinations (R.S. Aggarwal) - 10+2 standard arithmetic sections.
